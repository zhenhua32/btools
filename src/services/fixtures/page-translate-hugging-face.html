Video Prompt Writing Guide

This guide defines a stable schema for writing precise video-generation prompts. It explains how to describe subjects, actions, environments, cinematography, timing, and synchronized audio while preserving a clear separation between global and shot-level instructions.

1. Core principles

Describe only visible and audible details. Use concrete language, keep the chronology explicit, and avoid instructions that cannot be represented in the generated video.

2. Shot structure

Each shot should identify the camera framing, subject motion, environmental changes, and transitions. Dialogue and synchronized sound events belong with the shot where they occur.

3. Audio fields

The overall_soundscape field summarizes ambience and physical sounds across the complete video. Background music intended only for the audience belongs in non_diegetic_music. 

overall_soundscape: Quiet indoor room tone and a low ventilation hum continue throughout the video.

When reference audio is used, state its relationship only in the section matching the audible layer so that the prompt remains unambiguous and internally consistent.
